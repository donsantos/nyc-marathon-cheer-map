See them three times

  • Mile 8, Atlantic Ave / Barclays — huge hub, many trains.
  • Mile 17–18, 1st Ave (UES) — take the Q from Atlantic Ave straight up to 86th or 96th St, walk east.
  • Mile 23, 5th Ave ~102nd St — walk west from 1st Ave across 96th–102nd (about 15 min). Runners take ~50 min to loop through the Bronx, so there's time.
  • Finish — meet at Family Reunion on Central Park West (W 62nd–65th St).

Spectator tips

  • Track live in the free TCS NYC Marathon app (bib number or name). Splits post every 5K, so trust it over this sheet.
  • Agree ahead of time on which side of the street you'll stand. On 1st Ave, the west side is easier for getting back to the subway.
  • No spectators on bridges (miles 1–2, 13 into Queens, 15, 20–21) or on the post-finish stretch north of the grandstands.
  • Bring a sign or a bright balloon. Wave 5 crowds are big, and runners spot colors before faces.

Wave colors explained

Your race bib shows your wave (1-5), color (Orange, Blue, or Pink), and corral. The three colors take different paths on the Verrazzano Bridge and through Brooklyn: Orange runs on the upper deck left, Blue on upper deck right, and Pink on the lower deck. Blue and Pink merge at mile 3.2, while Orange runs on the left side of 4th Ave. All colors merge at mile 8 (Barclays/Atlantic Ave) and run together the rest of the way.

About these times

Times are the start time you enter above + (mile × pace). Many runners slow in the back half, so expect them nearer the 11:00 column after mile 20 (1st Ave → Bronx → 5th Ave hill). Locations are approximate cross streets, and the map is a schematic, not a street map. Confirm the wave start in the official app the day before.
